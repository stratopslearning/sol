# FERPA rider (institution template)

**Not legal advice.** Customize with counsel before use with customers.

This rider applies when Institution uses SOL to create, store, or process **education records** subject to the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), 20 U.S.C. § 1232g.

## 1. School official / service provider

Provider acts as a **school official** / service provider with a legitimate educational interest solely to deliver the SOL LMS services (enrollment, assessment, grading support, discussions, reporting).

## 2. Purpose limitation

Provider will use education records only to provide, secure, and improve the services for Institution, and not for Provider advertising.

## 3. Redisclosure

Provider will not redisclose education records except to subprocessors listed in the then-current subprocessor list (or successors performing equivalent functions), as required by law, or with Institution direction.

## 4. Subprocessors

Provider maintains a subprocessor list. Material changes that expand education-record processing will be communicated with reasonable notice.

## 5. Security

Provider maintains administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of education records, including access control, audit logging, encryption in transit, and provider encryption at rest.

## 6. Breach notice

Provider will notify Institution without undue delay after confirming a breach of Institution education records in Provider’s possession, and will cooperate on investigation and required notices.

**Institutional customization (e.g. FGCU):** Have counsel replace placeholders with named breach contacts, notification SLAs, and any state-specific education-privacy terms before signature. This file is a template only — not legal advice.

## 7. Student / parent rights

Institution remains the FERPA “educational agency or institution” for access, amendment, and hearing rights. Provider will assist Institution in fulfilling access, amendment, and deletion requests using product tools (including soft-delete and purge) within a commercially reasonable time.

## 8. Deletion / return

Upon termination or written request, Provider will delete or return Institution education records from production systems within the retention schedule, except limited backups retained per Provider’s backup policy until expiry, and except records Provider must retain by law.

## 9. AI processing

Institution acknowledges that short-answer grading and discussion features may transmit student-generated content to AI subprocessors under Zero Data Retention / no-training controls described in Provider’s AI education-records documentation.
