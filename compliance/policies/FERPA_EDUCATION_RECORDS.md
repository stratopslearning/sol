# FERPA / Education Records Handling

- Treat LMS academic data as education records ([`../DATA_INVENTORY.md`](../DATA_INVENTORY.md)).
- Access only with legitimate educational interest (role + section membership).
- Log disclosures and privileged views via `audit_log`.
- Do not redisclose outside Institution direction and approved subprocessors.
- Assist Institution with access, amendment, and deletion using soft-delete/purge.
- AI subprocessors only under documented ZDR/no-train posture ([`../AI_EDUCATION_RECORDS.md`](../AI_EDUCATION_RECORDS.md)).
- Customer contracts should attach [`../FERPA_RIDER.md`](../FERPA_RIDER.md).
