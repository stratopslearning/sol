## Executive summary

Hard-wall tenancy (RLS FORCE + `sol_app` without BYPASSRLS) is largely in place and the isolation suite is green. **Scoped role bindings are partially shipped** (`role_bindings`, permission catalog, staff invites, ownership transfer), but **`users.role` and `professorId` still dominate data-access gates** (~170 role compares / ~32 ownership compares + ~56 `professor_sections` joins; ~16 `authorize(` call sites). Several education tables still lack `tenant_id` and/or RLS. Product still runs **course + professor-owned activity** with offering columns bolted on.

**Blockers (fix before calling the model “done”):**
1. Remove `users.role` / `professorId` from authorize paths (or they remain two sources of truth).
2. Add `tenant_id` + FORCE RLS to `grade_overrides`, `regrade_requests`, `quiz_versions`, `audit_log` (and related knowledge satellite tables).
3. Retire live use of `DEFAULT_WORKSPACE_ID` (still present in DB and seed/helper paths).
4. Knowledge coordinator still grants power from **membership** `owner`/`admin` (`lib/knowledge/access.ts`) — contradicts seat-only membership.
