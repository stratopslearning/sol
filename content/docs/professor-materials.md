# Course materials

SOL Knowledge has three layers. The AI for a course only uses what you put in that course’s set.

## The layers

| Layer | What it is | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Course knowledge** | Sources attached to this course (syllabus, readings, old exams). Tutor, question generation, and rubrics pull only from here. | Teaching faculty for that course |
| **Shared library** | Workspace sources many courses reuse (textbook, policies, glossary). Courses **attach** items — they do not copy them. | Workspace faculty; coordinators manage the library |
| **Personal** | Your private drafts and notes until you share them into the library. | Only you (and workspace owners/admins) |

Open **Workspace → Knowledge** for Shared library and My private. Open a course **Materials** tab for that course’s attached set, uploads, and books.

## Rules that keep it safe

- **Attach, don’t dump.** A course only sees library items you explicitly attach.
- **Versioning with approval.** If a shared item changes, attached courses get a notice and can **Update** or **Keep** their pinned version. Nothing swaps mid-term silently.
- **Citations.** Tutor answers can cite whether a source is course material or shared library (excerpts only — not raw file downloads).
- **Invite to teach.** Professors only see courses they are invited to teach (faculty enrollment code on a section). Co-teachers share that course’s materials set. Workspace owners/admins can see all courses to invite and oversee.
- **No student data in Knowledge.** Do not upload grades, student work, or chats. Uploads ask you to confirm this.
- **Students** never browse the raw library unless you release authored content (lessons, books, quizzes).

## Prior course vs this term

Tag uploads and attachments as **Prior course** or **This term**. Authoring uses prior-course materials to rebuild last term’s structure; this-term fills updates. On rollover, SOL can clone attachments with their pinned versions; retag as needed.

## Add materials

1. Open the course **Materials** tab (only for courses you teach)
2. **Attach** a READY document from the **shared** library, or upload a file / zip / URL (zip expands into one material per PDF/DOCX/TXT/MD)
3. Optionally sync connectors under **Knowledge → Connections**, share into the library if needed, then attach
4. When a badge says **New version available**, choose Update or Keep
5. Wait until status is **Ready**

## Why materials matter

| Without materials | With materials Ready |
| --- | --- |
| Authoring has little to ground on | Draft outlines from your syllabus and notes |
| Tutor risks generic answers | Tutor answers from your course only |
| Concept tags may be thin | Stronger concept coverage for analytics |

## Prerequisites

1. Role is **Professor** (or Admin)
2. You teach the course (section invite) or are a workspace owner/admin
3. For shared-library admin actions, workspace **owner** or **admin**
