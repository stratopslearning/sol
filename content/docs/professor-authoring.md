# Authoring from materials

Authoring is a **chat + artifacts** workspace. You talk to SOL on the left; outlines and assignments open as **artifacts** on the right. Nothing is student-visible until you **Release**.

## What do I need before authoring?

1. Join a section for the course (faculty enrolment code)
2. Have at least one **Ready** material on the course ([Course materials](/docs/professor-materials)). You can add from Authoring (**Materials**) or the Materials tab
3. For a term rollover, tag last term’s syllabus and assignments as **Prior course** (uploads and library attachments). Leave new readings as **This term**.

## How do I author from materials?

1. Open **Authoring** — chat is the main surface; the artifact stage sits beside it (on mobile, open **Artifact**)
2. **Just prompt** when materials are Ready — SOL retrieves across the course corpus. Open **Materials** only to upload, attach from the library, or **focus** specific docs (checkboxes); leave unchecked to use all Ready
3. Send a short brief or tap **Propose outline** — SOL returns a **structure-only** skeleton as an outline artifact
4. Review modules in the **Outline** tab. **Approve & build** (artifact header or chat chip) fills quizzes, discussions, and practice. When prior-course materials are tagged, SOL **mimics** those assignments
5. Click an item to open the **Assignment** artifact. Optionally **Switch up**: same structure, new questions, with a similarity check in the **Diff** tab
6. **Refine in chat** in plain English (“make unit 3 shorter”). Focus follows the open assignment. Every change is a **revision** you can restore from the artifact header
7. Run the pre-publish **checks** banner (stale/missing dates, duplicates, inactive quizzes)
8. **As student** preview hides answer keys; **Instructor key** shows them — both replace the artifact body in place
9. **Publish (inactive)** then **Release to students**

## Tips

- Prefer clear syllabi and old quizzes/exports for prior-course tagging over dense scanned PDFs
- Re-propose archives the current draft; Refine edits in place
- Artifact chips in the thread jump you back to the outline or assignment
- Workspace **authoring standards** (structure, rubric style, tone) apply when set by a faculty admin
- Hand-built quizzes under **Assignments** still work; concept tags feed [Analytics](/docs/professor-analytics)
