# Lessons and flashcards

When your professor **releases** a lesson, it appears under **My Lessons** (or **Lessons** in the sidebar).

## Read a lesson

1. Open **Lessons** and pick a released item
2. Read the teaching notes your professor approved
3. Mark the lesson complete when you are done

Lessons come from your course materials through SOL’s authoring flow. Your professor edits and releases them; they are not anonymous web articles.

## Flashcards

If the lesson includes flashcards:

1. Flip each card to reveal the back
2. Work through the deck until the ideas feel familiar
3. Return later if your professor points you back before a quiz

## Tutor and lessons

Course tutors may use short excerpts from released lessons while coaching. They still will not reveal quiz answer keys, and they stay scoped to **your course**, not the open web.
