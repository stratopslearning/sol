# Student getting started

A short path from first account to your first quiz on SOL.

**Live app:** [https://www.strat-ops.net/learning](https://www.strat-ops.net/learning)

## 1. Create your account

1. Open [https://www.strat-ops.net/learning](https://www.strat-ops.net/learning).
2. Choose **Sign up** (or go to `/learning/signup`).
3. Use your **school email** when possible.
4. Complete email verification if prompted, then sign in at `/learning/login`.

After sign-in, open **Open your dashboard** — students land on the student dashboard.

## 2. Join your section

Your professor will share a **learner enrolment code** (syllabus, LMS post, email, or class slide). There is no public course catalog to browse into.

1. On your student dashboard, find the enrolment form.
2. Enter the **learner enrolment code** exactly as given.
3. Submit to join the section.

You should see the section listed under your enrolments. If the code fails, double-check that you received the **learner** code (not a faculty code) and that there are no extra spaces.

## 3. Find your coursework

Once enrolled:

| Place | What you’ll find |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Snapshot of enrolments and recent activity |
| **Quizzes** | Assigned quizzes — start or continue an attempt |
| **Discussions** | Socratic discussion bots assigned to your sections |
| **Grades** | Scores and feedback on submitted work |

Open a quiz when you are ready; follow any time limits or attempt rules your instructor set.

## 4. Payments (only at some institutions)

Some deployments ask students to complete enrolment payment before the full dashboard opens. If you see a payment screen, finish that step, then return to your dashboard. If payments are not enabled for your school, you can skip this entirely.

## Checklist

- [ ] Signed up with school email
- [ ] Signed in and opened the student dashboard
- [ ] Joined section with the learner enrolment code
- [ ] Can see at least one quiz or discussion (once your professor assigns work)

## Need help?

Ask your professor for the correct learner code, or contact your institution’s SOL administrator. Faculty setup is covered in [Professor onboarding](/docs/professor-onboarding).
