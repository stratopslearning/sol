# Brag Plan: SOL

## What is this app?
SOL is the operating system for teaching: an AI teaching layer that turns an instructor's own materials into courses, practice, a course-scoped tutor, hard-to-game assessments, and auditable grades, with the instructor approving everything.

## The angle
**Sol means sun.** The brand's own origin line, "A sun doesn't do the work for you; it lets you see," becomes the whole film. The video is a slow walk through SOL's oil-painting gallery, lit like a sunrise: it opens on the lamp-lit night desk (the late-semester grind) and the light rises scene by scene until it lands on the sunlit campus path. Each SOL loop step is presented as a numbered **plate** in a cream mat, like a page from an old art catalog. Specific to SOL because every frame is SOL's own paintings, palette, type, and copy. Nothing generic.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark, lamp-lit oil painting (`loop-grades-gaps.jpg`), slow push toward the glowing lamp. Large Instrument Serif: **"Sol means sun."** then a quiet italic line: *"A sun doesn't do the work for you."* The viewer wants the next line.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The light comes up.** Warm light floods from the lamp, the night desk gives way to the sunlit classroom (`classroom-morning.jpg`): *"It lets you see."* then the SOL mark + wordmark and the canonical tagline "The operating system *for teaching.*"
- **Three plates from the loop**, one by one, each a framed painting with a catalog label:
  - Plate I · "Materials become a course draft" (`loop-materials-draft.jpg`)
  - Plate II · "A tutor that only knows your course" (`loop-course-tutor.jpg`)
  - Plate III · "Assessments that reward understanding" (`loop-assessments.jpg`, already a vintage postage stamp)
- **The proof card.** A real SOL grade, explained the way the product explains it: "7 of 10" with rubric marks filling (3 of 4), "You covered 3 of the 4 rubric points." Then the trust line: "SOL drafts. *You decide what ships and what scores.*"

## Outro / punchline
Sunlit path toward the campus (`path-light.jpg`), slow push-in. **"Teaching, *in a better light.*"** SOL mark + wordmark, `teachwithsol.com`. The sun has fully risen.

## User flow worth showing
Upload materials → draft course (Plate I) → students get a course-only tutor (Plate II) → assessments (Plate III) → grade shown with rubric points, instructor stays in control (proof card). The brand guide forbids fake UI over the paintings, so the flow is carried by the plates, and the one product-UI moment (the grade card) sits on cream paper, never on the art.

## Tone
- Preset: polished (with cinematic lighting arc)
- Creative direction: "our oil painting retro vibe" — a vintage art-catalog / museum walk at sunrise. Warm grain, cream mats, serif plate labels.
- Interpretation: few scenes, long holds, slow single camera moves (push-ins, gentle drift). Confidence through calm. Soft crossfades and light wipes, never hard cuts.

## Format: square — 1080x1080 (LinkedIn feed)
## Duration: 24.0s (3.6 + 3.4 + 8.2 + 5.2 + 3.6)

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, restrained editorial bed with 3-4 soft organic accents.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` from 0s, volume ~0.3, fade in over the first ~1.5s (night), fade out over the last ~1.2s.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`, ~110 BPM. Music starts at video 0, so track time = video time.
  - Strong locks: wordmark ~4.91s (beat), proof heading ~17.47s (strong), final mark ~22.93s (strongest).
  - Beat grid for plates: 7.09 / 9.83 / 12.55 (every 5th beat, ~2.7s apart, so each title gets a full read).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. Music RMS/bass gently swells the warm light glow (lamp halo, sunlight wash). No visualizer graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse, warm (wood knocks, soft drops, one bell for the final mark).
- Restraint rule: nothing digital, glitchy, or clicky. No SFX on every beat.

### Scene 3 — The plates — 8.2s (7.0-15.2)
